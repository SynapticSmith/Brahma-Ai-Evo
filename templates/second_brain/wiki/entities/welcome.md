# Welcome to Your Second Brain

This vault is your personal knowledge base and autonomous agent memory layer.

## How to use this vault
1. **Raw Sources (`raw/`):** Save articles, transcripts, papers, or book notes here as append-only reference documents.
2. **Wiki Distillation (`wiki/`):** Ask Brahma to synthesize notes into `wiki/entities/`, `wiki/concepts/`, or `wiki/synthesis/`.
3. **Automatic Retrieval:** When you chat with Brahma (local model, custom API, or Gemini), Brahma automatically finds relevant notes from this vault and injects them into the conversation context.
